Example: Bring Your Own Device Policy

Task: In your group, discuss the possibility of introducing a "Bring Your Own Device" policy in your school. You may want to talk about:

  • the benefits and problems of this policy
  • how to prevent such problems
  • in which subjects mobile devices would be most useful for students
  • anything else you think is important

It is NOT recommended to talk about 'anything else you think is important'.

Q1 Pros and Cons:

Topic Sentence Elaboration Example
1. Enhance learning opportunities. Instant access to edu tools like interactive learning apps. Enrich understanding e.g. history and science.
2. Equity and distraction issues. Not all students have high-quality devices. Distraction during class persists. Over half of students use SM during class - disrupting focus.

Q2 Prevention:

Topic Sentence Elaboration Example
1. Provide devices for students who lack so. Ensure equal access to tech and edu resources. 1:1 device initiative - providing loan laptops to students.
2. Implement strict usage policy and tools. Clear guidance on acceptable use to keep students focused. GoGuardian allow teachers to appropriately monitor students.

Q3 Applicable Subjects:

Topic Sentence Elaboration Example
1. STEM subjects including Math and Science. Provide interactive simulations, coding apps for students. Khan Academy and Labster can offer virtual lab exp.
2. Language Arts such as English and Chinese. E-books and audiobooks can provide diverse learning styles. Grammarly can aid with writing assistance and Audible aids with listening.
